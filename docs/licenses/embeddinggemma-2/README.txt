EmbeddingGemma 2

Search by meaning runs EmbeddingGemma 2 by Google DeepMind
(https://huggingface.co/google/embeddinggemma-2), as converted to GGUF (Q8_0)
by ggml-org (https://huggingface.co/ggml-org/embeddinggemma-2-GGUF, revision
bfcd298762cc34d0357ece5ebdd31791a3a374d8).

The model is not included in Codiclens. When search by meaning is on, Codiclens
downloads it from Hugging Face to ~/Library/Application Support/codiclens/models
on your Mac. Google and ggml-org publish it under the Apache License 2.0; see
Google's model card at https://huggingface.co/google/embeddinggemma-2.
